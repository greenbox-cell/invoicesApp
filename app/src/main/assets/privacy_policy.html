Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Invoices (“the app”) is a local business invoicing tool. It is designed so that your invoice data stays on your device. We do not operate a backend, user accounts, advertising network, or analytics service for this app.

Information the app stores

When you create an invoice, the app saves the client name, date, amount, currency, item description, and paid/pending status in a local database on your phone or tablet. Generated PDF files are written to the app cache only so you can share them with the Android share sheet.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, email, or phone number is required.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone access is requested.
  • No advertising identifiers, crash analytics, or usage tracking SDKs are included.
  • Invoice contents are not uploaded to a developer server.

Sharing

If you tap Share PDF, Android’s system share sheet lets you send a copy to an app you choose (for example email or messaging). That copy then follows the privacy policy of the destination app. The Invoices app does not transmit the file itself.

Permissions

The app does not require INTERNET or storage permissions. Sharing uses Android FileProvider so other apps can read only the PDF you explicitly share.

Data retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until you delete an invoice, use Settings → Clear all invoices, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the local database.

Children

Invoices is intended for business users and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the in-app copy will be updated and the “Last updated” date will change.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email listed on the Google Play store listing for Invoices.
